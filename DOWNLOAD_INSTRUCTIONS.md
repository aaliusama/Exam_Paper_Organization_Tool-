## 📝 File Naming Convention

Files are named: `9709_[session][year]_[type]_[paper].pdf`

Examples:
- `9709_s23_qp_12.pdf` = 2023 Summer (May/June) Question Paper P12
- `9709_w21_ms_32.pdf` = 2021 Winter (Oct/Nov) Mark Scheme P32
- `9709_m22_qp_42.pdf` = 2022 March Question Paper P42

**Session Codes:**
- `s` = Summer (May/June)
- `w` = Winter (Oct/Nov)
- `m` = March (Feb/March)

**Type Codes:**
- `qp` = Question Paper
- `ms` = Mark Scheme
